# Privacy Policy — VETS Agent OS

**Bot:** VETS Agent OS ([@VETS_AGENT_OS_BOT](https://t.me/VETS_AGENT_OS_BOT))  
**Operator:** VETS / the HERO ecosystem, in support of VIC Foundation  
**Effective date:** 17 September 2026  
**Last updated:** 17 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VETS Agent OS (“the Bot,” “we,” “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Bot on Telegram, including private chats, groups, and inline mode.

This is not legal advice. Telegram also processes data under the [Telegram Privacy Policy](https://telegram.org/privacy) and, unless we publish a different policy, the [Standard Privacy Policy for Bots and Mini Apps](https://telegram.org/privacy-tpa).

## 1. Who we are

VETS Agent OS is a Marine-built AI assistant for veterans, first responders, and families. It provides task help and information related to the HERO token, [herobase.io](https://herobase.io), and the VIC Foundation mission (“Honor Through Action”).

Contact for privacy requests:
- Telegram: [@VETS_AGENT_OS_BOT](https://t.me/VETS_AGENT_OS_BOT)
- Website: [https://vicfoundation.com](https://vicfoundation.com)
- Related sites: [https://herobase.io](https://herobase.io) · [https://docs.vicfoundation.com](https://docs.vicfoundation.com)

## 2. What we collect

We only collect what Telegram sends us when you use the Bot, plus what you choose to send.

### From Telegram (automatically)
- Telegram user ID
- Username, display name, language code, and profile data Telegram provides to bots
- Chat ID and chat type (private, group, channel, inline)
- Date and time of messages and queries
- Inline-mode query text when you type `@VETS_AGENT_OS_BOT` in any chat

### From you (only if you send it)
- Message text, commands, voice notes, photos, documents, and other files
- Contact, location, wallet address, email, or other personal details you type or forward
- Feedback, support requests, and volunteer or donation information

### Affiliate / rewards
If you use Telegram’s affiliate program or related referral links for this Bot, Telegram may record referral and reward events. Any rewards associated with this Bot’s affiliate activity are intended to benefit the $HERO token and ecosystem. We do not ask Telegram for your payment-card details; payment processing is handled by Telegram where applicable.

### We do not collect
- Your phone number, unless you explicitly share a contact with the Bot
- Precise location, unless you send a location pin
- Private keys, seed phrases, or passwords (never send these to the Bot)

Do not send seed phrases, private keys, bank passwords, or government ID images to the Bot.

## 3. How we use information

We use this information to:
- Operate the Bot and answer your requests
- Remember context needed for a conversation or command
- Provide $HERO, herobase.io, and VIC Foundation information you ask for
- Improve reliability, safety, and features
- Detect abuse, spam, and security issues
- Attribute affiliate or referral rewards to the $HERO ecosystem when that program is used
- Comply with law if we are legally required to do so

We do **not** sell personal information. We do **not** use Bot chats to build advertising profiles for third-party ads.

## 4. AI and other processors

To generate answers, your prompts and limited Telegram identifiers may be sent to infrastructure and AI service providers that host or process the Bot. Those providers process data only to return a response or keep the service running.

Telegram, as the platform, independently processes data under its own policies.

On-chain activity (wallet addresses, token transfers, public $HERO transactions) is public by design on the relevant blockchain and is not controlled by this Policy.

## 5. Sharing

We share information only when:
- You ask us to (for example, you choose to post a result in a group)
- A service provider must process it to run the Bot (hosting, AI inference, logging)
- Telegram requires it to operate bots, inline mode, Stars, or the affiliate program
- The law requires it (valid legal process)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data.

## 6. Retention

- Operational logs and message content are kept only as long as needed to provide the service, debug issues, and meet legal duties.
- You may ask us to delete Bot-side data we control (see Section 8).
- Telegram may retain copies on its own systems; we cannot erase Telegram’s platform logs.
- Public blockchain records cannot be deleted.

## 7. Groups, channels, and inline mode

- In groups, the Bot may see messages depending on Telegram privacy-mode settings and whether it is an admin.
- Inline mode sends the text you type after `@VETS_AGENT_OS_BOT` to the Bot so it can return results. Other people in that chat see only what you choose to send.
- Do not put other people’s private information into the Bot without their permission.

## 8. Your choices

You can:
- Stop using the Bot and block it in Telegram
- Ask us for a copy of personal data we store about you
- Ask us to correct or delete personal data we store about you
- Remove the Bot from a group you administer

Send a request in Telegram to [@VETS_AGENT_OS_BOT](https://t.me/VETS_AGENT_OS_BOT) with the word **privacy** or **delete my data**, or use the contact options in Section 1. We may need to verify that you control the Telegram account. We will respond within 30 days where the law requires it.

Some data must be kept if the law requires it (fraud, security, tax, or legal claims).

## 9. Children

The Bot is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). Do not use it if you are under that age. If we learn we collected data from a child, we will delete what we control.

## 10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data we control. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Never send secrets (seed phrases, private keys, one-time codes) to the Bot.

## 11. International processing

The Bot may be operated and processed in the United States and other countries where our providers run servers. If you use the Bot from elsewhere, you understand your information may be processed outside your home country.

## 12. Changes

We may update this Policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use of the Bot after an update means you accept the revised Policy. Material changes will be noted in this document.
